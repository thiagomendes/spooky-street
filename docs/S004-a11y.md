Branch `feature/s004-a11y` (from `main` 71c7ca4). Not merged. Contract: `docs/S004-plan.md` §3 "Stream F2" (A11Y-01,

## What the child (and the grown-up) notices

- **Every screen shows where the gamepad or keyboard is.** The title, the pause card, the "Are you sure?" notices
  and the parent gate now use the same candy outline as the notebook, the settings card, the trunk and the language
  screen. Before, the title and the pause card used Unity's own selection, which showed nothing on the orange
  button.
- **The outline reads on paper.** The candy stitch now runs on a dark ink band, so it stands out on the notebook's
  cream page as well as on the dark felt tray (frame 81 showed it faint).
- **Cards stack.** A card opened over another one (settings over the pause card, the gate over the title, the
  language screen over the pause card) takes every press while it is up. When it closes, the card underneath gets
  its focus back where it was. This fixes the pause card, which had no gamepad focus after the settings card or the
  language screen closed. The press that closes a card never also acts on the card below.
- **Back closes the screen, and only the screen.** Escape on the notebook or the trunk closes it without also pausing
  the game. Back (Escape, East) now cancels the parent gate. On the title, Back starts nothing; it returns to the first
  button. On a notice, Back is the safe choice ("No").
- **Prompts per scheme.** With a gamepad or keyboard, the notebook's page discs carry the page buttons (the bumpers, Q
  and R), the close disc carries Back, and the Submit button rides the corner of the outline, so the child sees which
  button picks up and places the sticker the outline rings (frame 77). Touch shows none of them: the stickers and discs
  are the controls. No shared line says "tap", "drag", "WASD" or "stick" any more; a lint keeps it that way in English
  and Portuguese.
- **The grown-ups' pages say they scroll.** The paper fades at the bottom with a bobbing chevron while there is more
  below (with the page-down glyph on gamepad and keyboard), and fades at the top once scrolled.
- **Speech at any aspect ratio.** On an iPad (4:3) the speech bubble, the face disc and the next disc now shrink
  together to fit the width. Phones are unchanged.
- **Settings pictures.** Camera speed has its own picture (a camera with a turning arrow), and so does Vibration (a
  buzzing phone). Before, Camera speed shared the eye with Large text, and Vibration shared the knock rings with
  Sounds.
- **Owner's text decisions (2026-11-24).**
  - In Portuguese, skip is "Passar" (dialogue, cutscene, memory, "Aperte de novo para passar"), so it is never confused
    with Pip's Jump button ("Pular").
  - The Use disc reads the verb "Mergulhar" in the air, like "Rolar" and "Pular". Its paper tag is wider (184 units,
    was 150) so "Mergulhar" shows whole at full size; every other verb fits too. It is the one length-budget exception
    in `PortugueseTableTests`, with its reason; no other key may grow.
  - The bench hint names something a child can see, not a compass point: "Mr. Vale's bench is at the end of the little
    fence." / "O banco do Sr. Vale fica no fim da cerquinha." It still names only the area, and fits the caption strip
    in two lines (a longer first version with "the big tree" took three; run #192).
  - The jump hint says "Press Jump quickly to hop up!" / "Aperte Pular rapidinho para subir!" (was "Tap"/"Toque em").
  - New game already clears everything, keepsakes and costume included: no change.

## Sound off: every required signal has a visible equivalent

- **Clue sounds have captions** (`AccessibilityPassCoreTests`): the arrival knocks, the floor hum, the grille, the arm
  clicking in, the crank's gap and the owl's bows. Each is a bracketed sound caption in en.json.
- **The knocks and the floor hum take a direction word** when their source is away from Pip (unchanged, checked).
- **The cam taps and the wind wheel's whirr** pair with a motion or a glint on the thing itself, which reduced motion
  keeps at 30 %. The wind wheel is not a clue yet, so nothing depends on hearing it.
- **Haptics** carry nothing: the game plays none yet, and the switch only records the choice.

## A11Y-01 and MAP-01

- **A11Y-01** (`AccessibilityPassTests`, PlayMode): one whole case by gamepad only, one by keyboard only and one by
  touch only. The keyboard and touch runs play with sound muted, vibration off, large captions, solid caption boxes
  and reduced motion. Pip is placed at the Core route's stand points (`CaseStage`, `CaseRouteLayout`, the ramp route up
  to June's window and out by the door); the walk itself is MAP-01's `MandatoryRouteIsWalkableWithTheAuthoredColliders`.
  Everything else is the scheme's own press: New game, the opening skipped, every Use, Bea's lines read to the end
  and the others skipped, the finale read, the three deductions placed with the cursor (South/Enter) or by tap-tap,
  the notebook closed with Back, and the reveal and the seed skipped. The runs check that the knocks and the hum show
  as captions and that the settings hold to the end.
- **MAP-01 with the bats** (`AccessibilityPassCoreTests.Map01StillHoldsWithThePaperBatsPlaced`): no bat sits in or
  next to a threshold zone, the main loop's bats ask for nothing beyond a walk and a tap jump (the case never needs a
  bat), and a bat waits within 5 m of the bench, the treehouse and the arrival porch, so each has a second use.
