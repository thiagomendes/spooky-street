## Game assets

- **Pip:** `Models/Characters/Pip/Pip.fbx`.
  - 29.9k triangles, 39 bones and 12 in-place clips.
  - Baked textures: 2048 atlas, normal map, and a 1024 face map.
  - Review image rendered from the re-imported FBX: `pip_game_review.png`.
- **Environment:** `Models/Environment/`, 27 FBX files with two baked 2048 texture sets.
  - Owl house: 24.8k triangles. Its door, rooftop owl puppet and wind wheel are separate children so they can be animated.
  - Plus the house dressing, 16 props and 9 porch-kit modules.
  - Review image: `env_game_review.png`.
- **Engine side:**
  - `ModelImportRules` v4: Generic rig, cleaned clip names, looping clips by name, and textures forced to 2D.
  - `PipAnimator`: a Playables graph driven by the `PipPoseModel` core.
  - `ModelKit`: falls back to the shared atlas, gives emissive materials to `*_Glow` parts, and keeps the FBX root's axis
    rotation.
  - `EnvironmentDressing`: places the models from the engine-free `EnvironmentLayout`, with tests for the route and
    for detail density.
  - `AmbientMotion`: spins the wheel, sways the owl and bobs the paper bats; it freezes when the game is paused.
  - Night-violet sky, moon, and an attic window that stays the brightest glow.
