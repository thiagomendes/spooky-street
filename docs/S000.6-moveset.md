# S000.6: moveset core on Jump and Use

Branch `feature/s000.6-moveset`. Implements the first part of the spec v1.2 moveset (D14). The movement logic lives
in the engine-free `Core/Movement/MovementModel.cs`; tuning values are in `MovementTuningData` and
`Config/PipMovementTuning.asset`.

## Moves

| Move | Input | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| Variable jump (high jump) | Jump | A tap rises 1.0 m. Keeping Jump held 0.1 s after take-off engages a hold boost (a thrust up to 6 m/s held for 0.14 s) and the jump peaks at 2.1 m, then floats 0.12 s at the apex. Letting go during the boost lands in between. Same heights at 30 and 60 fps. The boost puffs paper stars under Pip's feet, flares the cape (stretch) and draws a short speed line. |
| Chain jump | Jump again within 0.35 s after landing, or up to 0.15 s before it, at ≥ 25 % of max speed (a light jog) | Held: 2.1 m, then 2.4 m, then 2.8 m (tapped: 1.0, 1.14, 1.33 m). The third jump plays an air flip (the Roll clip). Resets if Pip waits or slows down. |
| Bat-cape glide | Hold Jump while falling, at least 0.12 s past a jump's apex | Fall speed capped at 1.75 m/s, slightly more air control (0.8), Glide clip. Ends on release or landing. A fresh press spreads the cape at once (in a jump, once the apex hang has passed), except when a downward probe says Pip touches down within the 0.15 s buffer: then it stays a buffered (chain) jump. |
| Roll | Use on the ground at ≥ 60 % of max speed | 0.5 s burst at current speed + 1.5 m/s, 0.25 s cooldown. Can jump out of it while keeping speed. |
| Dive | Use in the air, from any jump or fall, including a glide (the cape folds into the dive) | Forward hop at 5.5 m/s, once per jump. Lands into a roll. |

If something can be interacted with, Use interacts instead of rolling or diving (via the `IUseTargetSource` hook) and
the button shows that action's verb.

## Input

| Platform | Jump | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Touch | Jump disc | Orange Use disc, always visible in play, with a "Roll" tag on the ground and a "Dive" tag in the air when there is nothing to interact with. A thumb can slide from Jump onto Use (glide into dive) without lifting |
| Keyboard | Space | E |
| Gamepad | South button | West button |

## Feel pass after TestFlight 11.1 (`fix/moveset-feel`)

Owner, on the phone: "Glide and roll I managed; the other moves I couldn't do or didn't understand how." So the
chain jump and the dive were hard to execute and to discover on touch, and the variable jump did not read.

| Value | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Chain window after landing (`chainJumpWindow`) | 0.2 s | 0.35 s |
| Jump buffer before landing (`jumpBufferTime`; also counts for the chain) | 0.12 s | 0.15 s |
| Chain speed gate (`chainJumpMinSpeedFraction`) | 50 % of max (2 m/s) | 25 % (1 m/s) |
| Fresh air press → glide | at once, even 0.1 s above the ground | at once, unless touchdown is within the 0.15 s buffer (probe below the feet): then it is a buffered jump |
| Dive from a glide | dived, but the glide-end event was missing | dives; the glide ends the same frame |
| Use tag in the air | "Roll" | "Dive" while a dive is available |
| Tap 1.0 m / hold 1.6 m | unchanged | unchanged |

The glide change fixes a real trap: on touch every press is briefly a hold, so a chain press 0.1 s before touchdown
spread the cape, the cape braked the fall and the landing came after the buffer had run out: no jump at all.

### Readable feedback (`MoveFeedbackRules` in Core, `Runtime/Player/MoveFeedback.cs`)

- **Chain jumps.** Take-off stretch grows per stage (−0.04, −0.09, −0.13) and the landing squash too (0.045, 0.08,
  0.11). The 2nd jump bursts 10 paper pieces at Pip's feet, the 3rd 16. Pieces are orange, purple, plum and bone
  confetti plus candy-corn stars (just above the bloom threshold, the highlight).
- **Held jump.** A small extra stretch (−0.07) eases in while Pip rises with Jump held.
- **Dive.** The cape snaps (stretch −0.08) and 2 waves of 4 bone and candy-corn paper streaks trail behind Pip.
- **Dive landing.** 7 felt dust puffs (wing tan and path brown) swell and drift out low.
- **Cost.** One pool of 40 small unlit, double-sided quads built at boot. No particle system, transparency or
  shadows, and no allocations per frame. A full pool recycles its oldest piece.
- **Pause and reduced motion.** Pieces run on game time, so pause freezes them. `AmbientMotion.ReducedMotion` skips
  every burst. Squash stays, because it is the character's own motion.

### First-time tips (`Core/Tips/MoveTips.cs`, `Runtime/UI/MoveTipView.cs`)

A paper tag with the control's ink glyph and a candy-corn strip sits above the Jump disc, or above the Use disc for
the dive. Each tip shows at most once ever. The record is kept in PlayerPrefs (`spooky.tips.shown.move.*`) behind
`ITipStore`, since there is no preferences store yet. The tag has no raycast targets. It flutters in over 0.22 s
(a 0.12 s cross-fade with reduced motion) and drops away when it ends.

| Tip | Key | Shown | Ends early when |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keep holding Jump to go much higher | `tip.move.hold_jump` | the first jump released while still rising | a jump is held to its apex |
| Jump again as you land! | `tip.move.chain_jump` | the first landing at ≥ 25 % speed (not a dive landing) | a 2nd or 3rd chain jump |
| Hold Jump while falling to glide | `tip.move.glide` | the first jump falling at 2.5 m/s past its apex without the cape | the cape spreads |
| Use in the air to dive | `tip.move.dive` | the first time Pip is airborne after having rolled once | a dive |

Rules (`TipScheduler`):

- **Timing.** One tip at a time. A tip lasts 3 s or until its move is performed, then a 2 s cooldown follows.
- **No queue.** A trigger that arrives while another tip is up, during the cooldown, or while the first "walk" prompt
  is on screen is dropped. The situation has to happen again.
- **Already learned.** A move performed before its tip ever appeared counts as learned, and that tip never shows.

## High jump pass after TestFlight 12.1 (`fix/high-jump`)

Owner, on the phone: "Only the high jump I still couldn't do, or didn't understand. For me, holding or tapping
quickly, the jump is the same. When I hold, the difference is that she comes down gliding." Two causes:

- **The hold barely changed the jump.** 1.0 m against 1.6 m, and the extra 0.6 m came from a cut gravity spread over
  the whole rise, so a held jump looked like a slightly floatier tap.
- **The cape opened right after the short rise**, so holding read as "glide".

The new jump keeps the tap exactly as the routes expect. A hold gets a sustained thrust early in the arc, then a
visible apex float, and only then can the cape open.

| Value | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Tap apex (`jumpApexHeight`) | 1.0 m (take-off 8.9 m/s, cut gravity 40 m/s²) | 1.0 m (take-off 7.1 m/s, plain gravity 25 m/s²); a press up to 0.1 s long is still a plain tap |
| Held apex (`jumpHoldApexHeight`) | 1.6 m, peak 0.36 s after take-off | 2.1 m, peak 0.48 s after take-off |
| How the hold adds height | lower gravity for the whole rise (released: 1.6× gravity) | hold boost: 0.1 s after take-off with Jump held (`jumpBoostDelay`), the rise ramps at 50 m/s² (`jumpBoostAcceleration`) back up to 6 m/s (`jumpBoostSpeed`) and holds it for 0.14 s in total (worked out from the heights), then plain gravity |
| Apex float (`apexHangTime`, `apexHangGravityScale`) | none | 0.12 s at ¼ gravity while Jump is still held after a boosted jump (Pip drops about 4 cm) |
| Glide start in a jump | as soon as Pip falls | only at least 0.12 s past the apex; with the hold carried from take-off: peak at 0.48 s, cape at 0.62 s |
| Chain scales (`chainJumpSecond/ThirdHeightScale`) | 1.25, 1.5 (held 2.0, 2.4 m; tapped 1.25, 1.5 m) | 8/7, 4/3 (held 2.4, 2.8 m; tapped 1.14, 1.33 m). The boost speed scales with √scale and the boost lasts 0.15 s and 0.17 s |
| Released during the boost | n/a | plain gravity from there: the height grows steadily with how long Jump was held |
| Ceiling hit or dive | cancelled the rise | also ends the boost (no thrust back into a ceiling) |

### What the player sees

- **Tap.** The same 1.0 m hop as before, a little softer at take-off.
- **Hold.** About a tenth of a second after take-off, Pip visibly speeds up again and shoots to twice the height. At
  the boost a small puff of 8 paper stars and confetti kicks out under her feet and two short vertical speed lines
  trail below. The cape flares up: a stretch kick of −0.12 on top of the held stretch (now −0.10, eased in faster),
  capped at −0.16 (about 1.42× tall). She hangs at the top for a beat, then the cape opens if Jump is still held.
- **Reduced motion.** No puff and no speed lines. The stretch stays, because it is the character's own motion.
- **Tip.** `tip.move.hold_jump` now reads "Keep holding Jump to go much higher". The owner's suggested "Keep holding
  Jump to jump much higher" is 37 characters, over the 36-character limit `MoveTipTests` sets for the tag.

## Safety and camera

- **Falling recovery.** The 1.4 s fall clock pauses while Pip glides over ground. A glide out over the void still
  returns Pip to safe ground.
- **Pause.** Pausing clears held buttons and stops all moves.
- **Camera.** In the air it ignores vertical motion within ±0.3 m, so jumps and glides do not bob the view.

## Not in this pass

Wall slide, wall jump and ledge grab need climbable geometry. `MovementModel` notes where they plug in.
