Branch `feature/s000.5-witnesses`. This pass covers presence and ambient life only; conversations and case facts
come in S002.

## In game

- **Loading.** `NpcAvatar` loads each character at native scale and builds a Playables mixer: loops cross-fade, and
  a Wave one-shot blends back into the loop.
- **Reaction to Pip.** `WitnessReaction` is engine-free and tested. Based on Pip's distance, a witness goes
  rest → wave once → talk → linger → rest, with hysteresis between 4 and 4.75 m. The head turns toward Pip, up to
  35°.
- **Placement** (in `EnvironmentLayout`):
  - Bea stands at (−7.2, 0, −1.3), facing the plaza. Her point lands on the attic window, and her bicycle stands
    beside her.
  - Mr. Vale sits on the bench at (8, 0, 0), facing the owl house door. His hip joint is 0.56 m against a 0.47 m
    seat.
  - The mandatory route stays clear.
