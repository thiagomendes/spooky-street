## 1. What shipped

### Diegetic clue attractions (`Core/Case/ClueAttractions.cs`, `Runtime/Case/ClueAttractionView.cs`)

Every physical clue calls for curiosity by itself. An attraction plays only while Pip is in its range and on its floor
(same side of the house walls), and only while its sticker is not found. Once the sticker is found it stops for good,
and the model goes back to its own pose. There is no UI arrow, no ring and nothing on the HUD.

| Clue (sticker) | Attraction | Rhythm | Range | Paired sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Silver cloth (`cloth`) | the scrap flutters in a gust (up to 16°) and glints twice | idle, 4.5 s loop | 9 m: the ramp landing, the shed roof, the deck | none yet (no flutter sound in the S003 set) |
| Sill thread (`sill`) | the thread lifts in the draught and glints | idle, 5 s | 5 m in the attic | none |
| Floor hum (`floor_hum`) | dust motes shimmer on the hall boards toward the grille and hop on each knock | each mechanism knock | 6.5 m: seen from the stair (the hum is found on reaching the hall floor) | `clue_cam_tap` |
| Wooden piece (`wooden_piece`) | the grille's slats of light flicker warm on each knock | each knock | 6 m in the hall | `clue_cam_tap` |
| Owl panel (`owl_panel`) | the painted panel rattles softly (2.2°) and its eyes catch the nook lamp | each knock | 6 m; stops once the panel swings open | `clue_cam_tap` |
| Loose arm (`loose_arm`) | the slipped striped arm wobbles (10°) | each knock | 6 m, once the panel is open | `clue_cam_tap` |
| Wind wheel (no sticker yet) | the rooftop wheel whirs up for 1.6 s as a wind gust starts a knock cycle (the first knock is at 0.45 s) | each wind cycle | 26 m outdoors; stops calling once the knocks' question is solved | `clue_wind_wheel` |
| Wooden owl (`owl_booth`) | the owl's eyes catch the porch lamp; the owl stays still | idle, 6 s | 10 m: the front yard and the porch | none |
| Foil streamer (`streamer`, the decoy) | the lavender foil twinkles in the lamp | idle, 5 s | 7 m | none |

- **The decoy gets an attraction too.** D26 §2.7: no signal may sort clues from non-clues, so the honest decoy calls
  for curiosity exactly like a clue.
- **Knock attractions use the mechanism's own knocks.** `WorkshopView.Knocked` fires on the same frames where `GameAudio`
  plays the cam tap. The wheel's whirr uses `WorkshopView.CycleStarted(true)`, the frame where the wheel's sound plays.
  The S003 sound wiring is unchanged.
- **Motion is a small offset on the real model.** It is laid over the model's own pose after its animation each frame
  and taken off when the attraction stops (`PoseOffset`). If someone else moved the model since the last frame (the
  ambient spin, the workshop swinging the panel open), that becomes the new base, so the workshop and ambient motion keep
  their poses.
- **Glints are a brief catch of light,** not a steady glow. Two quick flashes per loop, dark for over 80 % of it. They
  are unlit, at `GlowLevels.ClueGlint` 0.95, under the bloom threshold, and kept at 2.4 % of the view height so they
  read at phone size.
- **Pause and reduced motion.** Everything runs on game time, so the pause freezes it (a test holds the clock).
  Reduced motion keeps the light (glints) and scales every movement to 30 %.

### Hint retiering (D26 §6, `HintAreas`, `HintMoves`, `HintPresentation`, `HintGuide`, `HintGhostDemo`)

Hints answer "where can I look?" and "how do I get there?", never "what does it mean?".

- **Areas, not objects.** `HintAreas` is the authored area table: the front yard, the bench corner, the side yard, the
  attic, the hall, the workshop side of the hall and the arrival porch, plus four threshold areas (the bridge's end
  before June's window, the alcove inside it, the path below the porch, the hall inside the door).
  - Each objective's hints point to one area, matching its rewritten sensory lines.
  - An area behind the house walls gives way to the threshold area on Pip's side (D24.1).
  - Every area centre stays at least 1.5 m from every Use target, and every area is at least 2 m wide (Core test).
- **Tier 1 is an area nudge.**
  - Eight paper-thin leaves (drawn in code, maple and pumpkin tints) ride a breeze from Pip to the area. They end in a
    small swirl at its centre, never at its edge where the things stand.
  - A soft warm glow rises over the whole area and fades: an unlit pool on its floor (`GlowLevels.HintAreaGlow` 0.55)
    plus a dim warm point light.
  - The nearest witness in view glances toward the area for 2 s, not at the object.
  - It lasts 6 s, or until the objective changes. The star sparkle on the object is gone.
- **Tier 2 is the bats to the area.**
  - The paper bats fly from Pip to the area's ring, then circle the area once at 90 % of its radius, 2.2 m up.
  - Six bats a wave, two waves 4 s apart, at most 12 billboards.
  - The goal sticker over the object is gone.
  - Core tests: no circling bat and no swirling leaf comes within 0.7 m of any Use target's marker.
- **Tier 3 demonstrates moves only.**
  - `HintMoves` holds the authored move spots of case 01's skilled ascent, on the climb up while the hint leads to June's
    window: by the crate (a tapped jump), on the crate (a jump onto the awning) and on the awning (the held jump onto the
    shed roof).
  - Everywhere else there is no tier 3. `HintSession.MoveAt` caps the radio at tier 2, so it shows two pips and a third
    press flies the trail again.
  - The demo is the ghost hand on the Jump disc (touch) or the lit Jump button or key cap (D25 glyphs, unchanged): a
    tap for a jump, wall kick or ledge climb, a hold with the candy ring for the high jump and the glide.
  - Never Use, Talk or a deduction.
  - The ten per-objective action lines ("Examine the grille.", "Talk to June.", …) are retired. Five move lines replace
    them (`ui.hint.move.*`).
  - The three tier-2 lines that named an object are rewritten as area trail lines: "Follow the bats up to the attic.",
    "… down to the hall, by the front door.", "… back to the little workshop."
- **Helper.** Opt-in and off by default, as before. After a long stall it now offers only the tier-1 nudge, never a
  demo (D26 §6).
- **Stuck deduction.** The S002.5a musing with the missing clue's place is unchanged.

## 6. Gaps

- **Wind-wheel bonus sticker.** The wheel calls (it whirs before each wind cycle), but it has no sticker, examine target
  or comic bonus panel yet. Adding one changes the tray count and the questions' data. Until then its call ends when
  the knocks' question is solved.
- **Sounds.** The cloth, sill, owl and streamer attractions have no sound: the S003 set has no flutter, rustle or
  twinkle effect. They pair only where `GameAudio` already has a clue sound (the cam tap and the wheel's whirr).
- **Hint areas follow the objective,** whose lines were rewritten as area lines in S002.5a. Pointing instead at "the area
  holding an unfound clue for the earliest open question" (§6) would need new lines per area and stays a follow-up.
- **The owl-panel sticker becomes unfindable once the grille is examined.** Looking through the grille swings the panel
  open, so its examine target is no longer offered. Its attraction stops then, but the tray keeps a "?" for it. This
  is S002.5a data and was not changed here.
