# S000.1 — Visual and UX polish of the walking skeleton

**Why:** TestFlight 0.0.1 (3.1) opened on the owner's iPhone with every surface magenta and a UI the owner
judged far from the spec ("UI, UX terríveis"). Root causes: built-in Default-Material under URP, no rendered
check in CI, UI ignoring §5/§6, opening shot not meeting §1/§4.

## Delivered

| Area | Change | Files |
|---|---|---|
| Materials | Every renderer derives from committed URP Lit/Unlit bases; PlayMode check forbids Default-Material | `World/MaterialLibrary.cs`, `Art/Materials/*.mat`, `Tests/PlayMode/WalkingSkeletonTests.cs` |
| CI eyes | Rendered captures (software OpenGL) of title, play start, after walking, pause; fails on magenta or black frames; frames printed to the log | `Tests/Visual/*`, `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `tools/ci/extract_frames.py` |
| Art direction | Art bible (palette, light story, UI language) | `docs/art-bible.md`, `App/Palette.cs` |
| Light | HDR URP, code-built Volume (neutral tonemap, amber bloom, ink vignette), moonlight rim, amber pools, trilight ambient, fog | `World/Atmosphere.cs`, `Settings/URP-Mobile.asset` |
| Opening composition | Porch, candy bowl, framed porch lamp, path to the gabled owl house with glowing round attic window, lit ground-floor windows, skyline, sky card | `World/GreyboxBuilder.cs`, `World/MeshFactory.cs` |
| Pip | Hooded amber rain cape over a moth costume (antennae, wings with eyespots), idle/gait/antenna motion | `Player/PipAvatar.cs` |
| UI | Andika + Sniglet (OFL); paper cards and ink discs drawn in code; title left column; icon + verb thumb controls; pulsing stick ghost; first-prompt paper tag above the stick; paper-card pause; press feedback | `UI/*`, `Controls/TouchControls.cs`, `en.json` |
