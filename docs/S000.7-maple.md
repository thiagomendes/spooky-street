# S000.7: the maple reads as a tree

Branch `fix/maple-branches`. Owner feedback on the build: "Are these balls in the tree meant to be the branches? It
looks strange this way. Doesn't it deserve a better finish that looks like a tree?" The first maple's crown was six
smooth painted masses with leaf cards on them; from the treehouse deck it read as balloons.

## What changed

`Maple.fbx` is rebuilt by `tools/blender/env/maple.py` (art only; no C#, no placement change).

| Part | How |
|---|---|
| Trunk | Same lean and girth as before (the treehouse deck hole and braces still fit), five buttress roots, a burl, furrowed bark that runs up the trunk. A single column up to the fork at 4.2 m. |
| Limbs and twigs | Four limbs leave the fork, then space colonisation grows the secondary branches and twigs into seven crown lobes (seeded, deterministic). Radii follow the pipe model; every branch is a tapered tube closed to a point. |
| Leaves | 532 cut-paper maple leaves (five points, folded on the midrib, curled lobes): rosettes at the 67 twig tips, small sprays along the twigs, a few big dark cards hung inside for depth. Rust underneath, amber above, a few yellow at the top. No canopy masses and no alpha: the sky shows between the lobes. |
| Fallen leaves | The seven leaves round the roots stay, now cut from the same leaf kit. |

Game model: 10 874 triangles (2 362 wood, 8 512 leaves with their back faces; about 16.0k vertices after welding),
one mesh and one material. Previous model: 4 952 triangles.

## Textures

The maple leaves the shared `Env` atlas and has its own `Maple_BaseColor/_Normal/_Emission.png` (2048), which
`ModelKit` picks up by name. `Env_*` is untouched, so no other prop was re-baked; the old maple's texels in it are
simply unused. The crown is 532 instances of 24 baked kit leaves (8 colours x 3 folds) that share their UVs, so the
leaves get 316 px/m and the trunk and limbs 253 px/m (the old maple had 36 px/m). Twigs are baked at 101 px/m.

Export: `blender -b -t 2 --python tools/blender/env/env_export_maple.py` (report in `maple_manifest.json`).
`env_export.py` no longer lists `Maple`; `TreehousePlatform` and `Hedge` still count 3 444 and 4 196 triangles.

## Gameplay frame

- Origin at the trunk base, trunk line unchanged. Bounds x -3.68..3.99, y -2.3..2.5, top 7.7 m (before: x -3.48..3.91,
  y -2.0..2.5, top 7.9 m). The trunk collider is unchanged; the root toes reach about 0.9 m at ground level and
  are under 0.25 m high beyond the collider.
- `TreehouseCabin` (branch `feature/s002-interior`): the first maple's two low boughs crossed its walls and door. Now
  the trunk is one column through the cabin, the limbs leave through the roof's ridge slot, and `maple.blocked`
  keeps branches and leaves out of the cabin, off its roof and out of the approach to its door. A sampled check of
  every branch segment and leaf against the cabin volume finds no intersection.
