## 1. What was built

- **`Assets/SpookyStreet/Content/Resources/Localization/pt-BR.json`** (+ meta):
  - schema 2, locale `pt-BR`, native name "Português (Brasil)";
  - the same 227 keys as English, in the same order, with English's translator contexts;
  - both plural keys have `one` and `other`.
- **No code was needed to ship it.** The built-in slot picks the table up. A Brazilian device starts in pt-BR
  until the player chooses, and the language screen shows it as "Já vem no jogo" with a Use button and no Remove.
- **Voice and register.**
  - The UI and the radio use the standard você imperative ("Siga", "Escute", "Segure").
  - The characters speak warm, spoken Brazilian. Bea is quick and exclaims. Mr. Vale is polite and old-fashioned
    ("Sente-se", "Eu a via", "jamais", "Ora, ora"). June whispers and is informal ("Escuta…", "Psiu", "batucando").
    Kit is theatrical ("Bem-vinda de volta, detetive!").
  - Pip is a girl, so lines addressed to her use feminine forms.
  - "Bows" is "reverências", so the refrain reads "Três batidas, três reverências".
  - The Jump button's label "Pular" is the word the move tips and level-3 hints use ("Segure Pular…",
    "Toque em Pular…").
- **Hints keep exactly the English vagueness.** For example, "Listen low down in the hall." became "Escute lá
  embaixo, no corredor." It says nothing about under the floor. No hint names June or the cape (the lint checks
  this).
- **Playtest kit.** `docs/playtest/c01-playtest-kit.pt-BR.md` Appendix A now matches the game word for word. The
  kit's wording was kept where it was good. It changed where the game's wording is better or where the kit's line
  went over the length budget:
  - Mr. Vale's two lines;
  - "Alumínio brilhante, faz crec.";
  - "beirada da janela" instead of "peitoril";
  - the owl lines;
  - the foil-streamer note;
  - the "Escute" hint;
  - the move hints with "Pular".

  A note at the top says the game now ships in pt-BR, so the appendix is only needed for an English build.
- **Speech bubble layout fix (`DialogueView`).** Run 174's frame showed Mr. Vale's 61-character line cut after
  "Ficou". The 60-unit text strip under the vignette could not hold two lines at the 26-unit minimum size. The cause
  was the layout, not the language: English lines over about 57 characters were cut too, and June's longest line
  has 84 characters. The bubble is now 351 units tall (it was 327). The strip is 86 units tall, so two lines fit,
  and the stage keeps its size. `DialogueView.BubbleLine` exposes the text for tests.
- **`tools/l10n/review_page.py`**: the owner review page generator (D2 scope in the plan).

## 2. Tests

- **Core, `PortugueseTableTests` (8 tests).** They run in `tools/core-tests` and in Unity EditMode.
  - `PackValidator.ValidateText` passes with **100 % coverage** and the same key set as English. The validator
    checks UTF-8, schema, locale, placeholders, rich-text tags, `string.Format`, plural forms and font coverage.
  - pt-BR is the shipped built-in slot.
  - Every text is translated, except the names and marks that stay, and the contexts are English's.
  - **+35 % budget.** Each text and plural form is no longer than its English text after the pseudo-localizer (+35 %
    plus two brackets), which is the length the layouts are proven against. All 227 pass.
  - Plural forms are one and other, and "{0} figurinha" / "{0} figurinhas" are picked by count.
  - **Fear-word lint.** No word of perigo, monstro, morto, morte, medo, assust…, terror, sangue, caveira, fantasma,
    pesadelo and the rest appears anywhere.
  - **Conclusion lint (CASE-04 in pt-BR).** Captions, Pip's looks, clue notes and tags, story-scene lines and hints
    contain no "porque", "por isso", "a resposta", "resolvid…", "entrou por", "foi a june", "significa", "deve ser"
    and the like. Hints never name June or the cape. The opening never mentions batida, June, prateado, coruja,
    brilho or toc.
  - The names follow the owner's decision, and the tips name the Jump button by its label.

## 5. Wording the owner should check (also marked UNSURE on the review page)

- **`ui.hud.dive` "Mergulho".** It is a noun, while "Rolar" is a verb. "Mergulhar" is over the 8-letter budget of
  that tag. If the owner prefers the verb, check the HUD at that width.
- **Skip is "Pular", the same word as Jump.** These are `ui.dialogue.skip`, `ui.cutscene.skip` and
  `ui.memory.skip`. Both are standard, and the buttons sit in different places with different icons. The
  alternative for skip is "Passar".
- **"Botões de ombro"** on the parent gate for gamepads. It is the neutral term, but a parent may know "LB/RB" or
  "L/R" better.
- **"Vigia"** for Bea's "lookout", and **"teatrinho"** for Kit's puppet booth. The kit prose says "barraca".
- **`c01.hint.check_view.locate` "lado leste"**, as in English. A 6-year-old may not know "leste" in either
  language.
